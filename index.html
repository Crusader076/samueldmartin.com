Construction Management

BearingCPM

Founder & Builder · Product + Engineering

Enterprise scheduling power at a small-firm price. BearingCPM gives the 5-person GC the same critical-path depth that $50M firms pay consultants for — a full WBS scheduling grid, earned-value financials, resource leveling, and an AI orchestration console. One flat price. Zero IT required.

Feature set

  • WBS scheduling grid with a deterministic CPM engine — durations, dependencies & float recalculated instantly
  • Earned-value financials: baseline budget (PV), earned value (EV) & actual cost (AC) with progress invoicing
  • Resource loading histogram & subcontractor leveling across trades and concurrency peaks
  • AI Orchestration Console — auto-provisions WBS, re-estimates lags, pings contractors
  • Baselines, look-ahead, daily reports & reusable project templates
  • "Site Verified Feed" for field evidence; automated G702/G703 pay apps
  • Bilingual (EN/ES), offline-capable field app

Agile / SAFe Tooling

PI Planning Studio

Founder & Builder · Product + Engineering

Plan, Align, Deliver. A collaborative SAFe PI Planning platform built for real-time, multi-team planning — team boards, story maps, an ART board, capacity-aware iteration planning, and confidence voting, all in one shared session.

Feature set

  • Team Planning Board with drag-and-drop stories mapped across iterations (I1–I3)
  • Live load vs. capacity bars per iteration to prevent over-commitment
  • Committed / uncommitted PI Objectives with business-value tagging
  • Risk board (ROAM), Story Map, ART Board & program backlog
  • Confidence Vote for team and ART-level alignment
  • Shareable team codes for real-time multi-user sessions
  • "My Classes" mode — built to teach and run SAFe PI Planning

Consumer / Gaming

HQ Companion

Creator · Product + Engineering

A beautifully art-forward companion app for players of the classic HeroQuest board game. It digitizes the bookkeeping side of tabletop play — champion sheets, stats, gold, and equipment — behind a premium dark-fantasy interface, so the table stays focused on the adventure. A polished passion project that shows range beyond the enterprise.

Feature set

  • Illustrated champion sheets with editable Body / Attack / Defend / Mind stats
  • Interactive stat orbs with max values, plus a live gold counter
  • Drag-and-drop equipment grid — equipped vs. empty slots at a glance
  • Inventory, treasure draws, blacksmith & alchemy shops, quest & party tracking
  • Shareable read-only character links
  • Installable, mobile-app-shaped PWA experience
